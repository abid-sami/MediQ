# 🏥 MediQ

### Connected Healthcare. Simplified Care.

**MediQ** is an advanced hybrid healthcare and hospital management platform designed to bring essential healthcare services into one connected ecosystem.

From emergency ambulance requests and live tracking to doctor appointments, hospital bed availability, digital prescriptions, diagnostics, pharmacy services, blood bank management, and billing, MediQ aims to make healthcare services easier to access and manage.

---

## ✨ Overview

MediQ connects patients, doctors, hospitals, laboratory staff, pharmacists, nurses, blood bank staff, receptionists, and ambulance drivers through a unified digital healthcare platform.

The platform combines **patient services, hospital operations, emergency response, and healthcare management** into one system.

### Core Services

* 🚨 Emergency SOS & Ambulance
* 🚑 Live Ambulance Tracking
* 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Appointments
* 🏥 Hospital Information
* 🛏️ Live Bed Availability
* 🗺️ Interactive Hospital Ward Map
* 🔬 Laboratory & Diagnostics
* 💊 Pharmacy
* 🩸 Blood Bank
* 📋 Digital Prescriptions
* 💳 Billing & Payments
* 🔔 Healthcare Notifications
* 📊 Healthcare Analytics

## 🚨 Emergency SOS

MediQ provides an accessible SOS system for requesting an ambulance.

Users can provide:

* Name
* Phone Number
* Current Location
* Emergency Type

After an emergency request is created, the system can display:

**Request → Ambulance Assignment → Live Tracking → Hospital Arrival**

The platform is designed so emergency information can be managed through the appropriate authorized users.

## 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Appointments

# 👥 User Roles

MediQ contains nine primary roles.

### 👑 Super Admin

Complete platform-level control and monitoring.

### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor

Appointments, consultations, medical records, prescriptions, laboratory requests, diagnostics, and follow-ups.

### 🧑‍💻 Patient

Appointments, medical records, prescriptions, laboratory reports, pharmacy, blood bank, ambulance, and billing.

### 🧾 Receptionist

Patient registration, appointments, check-in, doctor queue, admissions, beds, billing, and emergency arrivals.

### 👩‍⚕️ Nurse

Assigned patients, wards, beds, vitals, nursing notes, medication tasks, and patient alerts.

### 🔬 Laboratory Staff

Test orders, sample collection, laboratory processing, results, and reports.

### 💊 Pharmacist

Prescriptions, pharmacy orders, medicines, inventory, stock, and expiry management.

### 🩸 Blood Bank Staff

Blood inventory, blood requests, donors, donations, and reservations.

### 🚑 Ambulance Driver

Emergency assignments, pickup locations, navigation, live location, trip status, and trip history.

   │       ↓

   │       ↓

   │

   │       ↓
   │   Blood Bank
   │

# 🔐 Privacy & Security

Healthcare information is sensitive. A production implementation should use appropriate authentication, authorization, access controls, audit logging, secure data handling, and applicable healthcare/privacy requirements.

Access to medical records and other sensitive information should be limited according to the user's role and authorization.
